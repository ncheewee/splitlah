Privacy Policy — SplitLah

Last updated: July 17, 2026

SplitLah ("the app") is a simple trip expense-splitting tool. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information we collect

When you create or join a trip, the app stores the following on our server so that everyone in the trip can see the same data:

  • The trip name and the display names of people you add to the trip
  • Expense entries you add: amounts, currencies, categories, descriptions, and who paid
  • Optional PayNow/payment details you choose to enter for settling up
  • Optional feedback messages you submit, along with the screen you were on and app version, to help us fix bugs

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your real name, email address, or phone number unless you choose to type it into a field yourself (for example, a payment reference).

How this information is used

Data is used only to run the app's core function: keeping trip members' expenses in sync so the app can calculate who owes whom. Feedback is used only to improve the app.

Where data is stored

Trip data is stored in a Postgres database (hosted by Neon) and accessed through a small API (hosted on Cloudflare Workers). We do not sell, rent, or share this data with advertisers or data brokers, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Data retention and deletion

Trip data is kept for as long as the trip exists. If you'd like a trip or your data removed, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

Sharing with third parties

We use Cloudflare (hosting the API) and Neon (hosting the database) as infrastructure providers to run the app. We do not share your data with any other third party.

Children's privacy

SplitLah is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
